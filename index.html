    How to play

    You get two foods, each in an everyday portion. Tap the one with more protein.

    There are ten match-ups a day. They start easy and end close, they are the same for everyone, and new ones arrive at midnight.

    Most match-ups put a plant food against an animal food, and the plant wins about half of them: three or four of the seven each day. Every portion shows its weight, and each food is measured as its name says, so beans, lentils, rice and pasta are cooked.
